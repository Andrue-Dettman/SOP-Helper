# Packing completed assemblies

Fictional demonstration material. This is not an employer procedure or certified operational guidance.

## Before you start {#preparation}

[prerequisite:pack-pre-01 steps=*] Use completed assemblies with the sample completion record.

## Check and pack {#check-and-pack}

[warning:pack-stop-01 steps=pack-01] If an assembly or label does not match the completion record, stop packing it and contact the demo supervisor.

1. [step:pack-01] Match each completed assembly ID to the sample completion record.
2. [step:pack-02] Count the completed assemblies in whole units.
3. [step:pack-03] Place the confirmed assemblies in their assigned sample packaging.
4. [step:pack-04] Attach the sample assembly label and record the packed count.
