# Putting labeled stock away

Fictional demonstration material. This is not an employer procedure or certified operational guidance.

## Before you start {#preparation}

[prerequisite:putaway-pre-01 steps=*] Have the accepted, labeled stock record and sample location list available.

## Confirm the storage location {#confirm-location}

[warning:putaway-stop-01 steps=putaway-02] If the assigned location is missing or blocked, stop. Do not choose another location. Ask the demo supervisor.

1. [step:putaway-01] Match the container's part ID to the sample location list.
2. [step:putaway-02] Confirm that the assigned location label matches the list and is available.
3. [step:putaway-03] Place the accepted container in the confirmed sample location.
4. [step:putaway-04] Record the location in the sample putaway record.

## Terms {#terms}

Putaway record: the fictional record connecting an accepted container with its assigned location.
