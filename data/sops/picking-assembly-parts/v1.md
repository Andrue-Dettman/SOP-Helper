# Picking assembly parts

Fictional demonstration material. This is not an employer procedure or certified operational guidance.

## Before you start {#preparation}

[prerequisite:pick-pre-01 steps=*] Have the selected assembly ID, requested whole-unit quantity, and sample picking list.

## Pick the listed parts {#pick-listed-parts}

[warning:pick-stop-01 steps=pick-02] If a listed part is missing or insufficient, stop the affected pick. Do not substitute a part. Follow the reporting-shortages procedure.

1. [step:pick-01] Confirm the selected assembly ID and requested quantity on the sample picking list.
2. [step:pick-02] Match each listed part ID and required quantity to its labeled stock.
3. [step:pick-03] Place each confirmed required quantity in the assembly's sample picking container.
4. [step:pick-04] Compare the picked part IDs and quantities against the complete picking list.

## Terms {#terms}

Picking list: the fictional list of parts and quantities required for the selected assembly.
