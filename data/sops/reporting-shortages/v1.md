# Reporting a parts shortage

Fictional demonstration material. This is not an employer procedure or certified operational guidance.

## Before you start {#preparation}

[prerequisite:shortage-pre-01 steps=*] Have the sample build-readiness result and its dataset snapshot ID available.

## Record and report the shortage {#report-shortage}

[warning:shortage-stop-01 steps=*] Do not substitute parts, alter stock, or promise a build when required parts are missing. Unknown availability is not zero stock.

1. [step:shortage-01] Record the selected assembly ID and requested whole-unit quantity.
2. [step:shortage-02] Copy the known required, available, and shortage quantities from the sample build-readiness result.
3. [step:shortage-03] Mark any unknown availability as unknown rather than entering zero.
4. [step:shortage-04] Give the result and dataset snapshot ID to the demo supervisor and wait for instructions.

## Terms {#terms}

Shortage: a known required quantity minus known available stock, with a minimum of zero. Unknown availability leaves the shortage unknown.
